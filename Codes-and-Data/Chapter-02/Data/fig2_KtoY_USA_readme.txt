Figure 2: Capital to output ratio in the U.S., 1929-2023
Source: FRED
Calculated as the ratio of two series:
Numerator: Current-Cost Net Stock of Fixed Assets and Consumer Durable Goods (K1WTOTL1ES000), Annual, Not Seasonally Adjusted, converted to billions of dollars (1925-2023)
https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/K1WTOTL1ES000 
Denominator: Nominal GDP (GDPA), Annual, Not Seasonally Adjusted, reported in billions of dollars (1929-2024)
https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/GDPA 
The figure plots the ratio between fixed capital and consumer durables relative to the GDP.
